# Blog editorial workflow

Articles are general patient education, not individualized diagnosis or a place
to make treatment promises. The content strategy titles are research starting
points only; no article is pre-approved.

## Draft and publication requirements

Editors may save incomplete articles as drafts. Publication is blocked until the
article has all of the following:

- an identified author;
- constrained body content;
- at least one factual source with a direct URL;
- `Reviewed` status;
- an identified medical reviewer; and
- a completed review date that is not in the future.

Featured images require meaningful alternative text. A next-review date is
optional, but when supplied it must be after the completed review date. Wagtail's
first-publication timestamp provides the displayed publication date.

## Recommended operating sequence

1. Create the article as a draft and establish its distinct search intent.
2. Research and attach direct, reputable sources.
3. Add original educational copy without personalized diagnosis or guarantees.
4. Record the real author and assign the actual reviewer.
5. Complete medical review, enter the true review date, and mark it Reviewed.
6. Preview links, images, headings, source URLs, and related content.
7. Publish only after clinic approval; set a future review date when appropriate.

Related treatment and article cards appear only when the target pages are live
and unrestricted. No author, category, article, source, or review claim is seeded
by migrations.
