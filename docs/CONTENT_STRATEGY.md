# Initial content strategy

Every medical article requires factual sourcing, an identified author, and a
genuine review state before publication. Titles are starting points for research,
not claims or pre-approved copy. Link each article to relevant published
treatment, doctor, and clinic pages only when the relationship is real.

## Editorial requirements

- Establish search intent and avoid overlapping articles competing for the same
  query.
- Use plain language, descriptive headings, original clinician input, and links
  to reputable medical sources.
- Display publication and last-reviewed dates accurately.
- Add an author/reviewer only after their actual participation.
- Avoid diagnosis, fear-based copy, guarantees, invented statistics, and forced
  geographic keywords.
- Review time-sensitive medical guidance on a documented schedule.
