## Privacy notice draft

### Who this notice covers

This notice explains how the website for Dr. Naresh Rathod, Dolphin Derma Care
in Sitapura, and Arya Skin and Hair Clinic in Chaksu handles personal
information. For a privacy request or complaint, contact the clinic using the
phone number or email shown on the Contact page and identify the request as a
privacy matter for Dr. Naresh Rathod.

### Information collected

If a visitor submits an appointment request, the website collects the selected
clinic, name, phone number, optional email address, preferred date and time,
contact consent, request status, and submission/update times. The visitor should
not enter a diagnosis, detailed medical concern, medical history, or emergency
information because the form does not request or require it.

The website also uses strictly necessary session and security cookies. Normal
technical requests may process network and browser information needed to deliver
and protect the service. Application logs record method, path, response status,
and errors; they exclude query strings, form bodies, and appointment fields.
Some public styles or scripts may be delivered by an infrastructure or content
delivery provider, which receives ordinary request metadata such as IP address
and browser information.

### Why information is used

Appointment information is used only to route the request to the selected
clinic, contact the requester, manage the appointment enquiry, prevent abuse,
and maintain service security. Submitting the form does not confirm an
appointment and does not create an electronic medical record or patient portal.

### Consent and choices

The appointment form is submitted only after the visitor agrees that the
selected clinic may use the supplied details to contact them about that request.
The visitor may withdraw that consent or request access, correction, erasure, or
grievance handling through the published clinic contact route, subject to any
information the clinic must lawfully retain. If the requester is under 18, a
parent or lawful guardian should submit and manage the request.

### Sharing and security

Dr. Naresh Rathod is the designated enquiry monitor. Access is restricted to
authorized administrator accounts; a technical administrator may access
information only when necessary to maintain or secure the service. Information
may also be processed by hosting, backup, or technical providers strictly to
operate and secure the website, or disclosed where legally required. It is not
sold. Reasonable access controls, encryption in transit, restricted
administration, backups, and security logging are used, but no internet service
can promise absolute security.

### Retention

An appointment enquiry is kept while it is being handled. After it is marked
closed, it is retained for 90 days and then deleted. A deleted record may remain
in a restricted local backup for up to 14 additional days before that backup
expires. Security and operational records are kept only as needed for operation,
investigation, and legal obligations.

### Analytics and changes

Optional analytics is currently disabled. If analytics is introduced later,
this notice and the website's consent controls must be updated before it is
enabled. This notice may be revised when website practices or legal requirements
change; the displayed review date will identify the current version.

## Medical disclaimer draft

Information on this website is for general education and clinic information. It
is not personalized medical advice, diagnosis, a prescription, or a substitute
for examination and advice from a qualified healthcare professional. Reading
the website or sending an appointment request does not by itself create a
doctor-patient relationship.

Do not use this website or appointment form for an emergency. Seek urgent local
medical assistance when symptoms may be serious or time-sensitive. Do not delay
professional care because of information read here.

Conditions, investigations, procedures, suitability, expected results, risks,
recovery, and costs vary between individuals. A clinician can discuss options
only after an appropriate assessment. The clinic does not promise a cure,
guaranteed outcome, permanent result, or suitability of any listed service for a
particular person.

An appointment request records a preferred clinic, date, time, and contact
details only. It is not confirmed until the clinic responds. Do not submit a
diagnosis, detailed medical history, reports, photographs, or emergency details
through the form.

The clinic aims to keep published information accurate and reviewed, but medical
knowledge, services, schedules, and external resources can change. Links to
third-party websites are supplied for convenience and do not make the clinic
responsible for their content, availability, or privacy practices.
